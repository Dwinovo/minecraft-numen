# Style: elven_nature — 精灵自然(树屋)

Grown, not built: curves, living wood, canopy platforms.

## Proportions
- platforms r=3-5 discs (`cylinder` height 1) at 2-3 heights around a trunk; huts 5x5 walls 3
- nothing perfectly straight: stagger edges by 1 randomly (`scatter` the rim)

## Palette
- living: oak/spruce logs, moss_block, azalea_leaves/flowering canopy (`sphere` hollow tops)
- floors: stripped_oak; rails: fences + vines; light: glow_berries? use lantern + shroomlight

## Recipe
1. trunk: `cylinder` r=1-2 oak_log, tall; canopy: 2-3 offset hollow `sphere` tops of leaves
2. platform discs on the trunk; spiral walkway: successive 2x1 `box` steps winding up
3. huts on platforms with leaf `roof`; `scatter` moss_carpet + flowers over every platform
