# cyberpunk — 赛博朋克

A dense grey city with neon nailed to it. The buildings themselves are cheap
concrete boxes stacked too close together; what you actually see is the layer of
signage, cables, ducts and glowing colour bolted onto their faces. Everything is
vertical, wet-looking and overcrowded, and the light does all the work — the
architecture is the dark backdrop it plays against.

## Materials
- **base structure**: gray_concrete, black_concrete, polished_blackstone,
  deepslate_bricks — deliberately drab
- **neon**: any bright concrete or glazed_terracotta backed by glowstone or
  sea_lantern; magenta, cyan, purple and red carry the palette
- **glass**: tinted_glass, black_stained_glass, light_blue_stained_glass
- **clutter**: iron_bars, chain, hopper, item_frame signage, banners as hanging
  ads, scaffolding
- **light**: sea_lantern, glowstone, redstone_lamp, shroomlight — abundant and
  coloured, never white
- **wet ground**: black_concrete, polished_blackstone with water puddles

## Proportions
- towers 5–12 times their base width, crammed with 2–4 blocks between them
- signage and neon cover 0.3–0.5 of the visible facade
- overhead: bridges, pipes and cables crossing the street at several heights
- street level dark and narrow; the sky is a slot

## Signature moves
- **Neon over grey** — the building is dull, the signs are not. Keep the
  structure desaturated so the colour reads.
- **Vertical density** — stack, overhang and bridge between towers until the sky
  is barely visible from the street.
- **Cable and duct sprawl** — chains and bars running diagonally between
  buildings at multiple levels.
- **Holographic signage** — large flat coloured panels mounted proud of the wall,
  lit from behind.

## Variants
- **street level** — market stalls, rain, crowded alley, food stands
- **corporate tower** — cleaner, monolithic, one enormous logo
- **slum stack** — shipping-container housing bolted to a structural frame
- **rooftop** — antennas, AC units, neon seen from above, a helipad

## Avoid
- warm daylight; this style lives at night
- clean uncluttered facades
- historical ornament of any kind
