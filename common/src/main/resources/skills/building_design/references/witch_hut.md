# Style: witch_hut — 女巫小屋

Crooked, overgrown, slightly wrong on purpose.

## Proportions
- small 7x7 walls 3, but roof TOO tall (5-6) and slightly offset by 1 to look crooked
- stilts: raise the whole hut 2-3 on log legs

## Palette
- dark_oak + spruce, mossy everything (mossy cobble foundation), roof dark_oak
- accents: mushroom blocks, cobwebs?? use `scatter` brown_mushroom; light: soul_lantern

## Recipe
1. legs: 4 `set` columns of dark_oak_log; platform `box` above them
2. hut `walls` + oversized offset `roof` (base rect shifted 1 to one side)
3. porch + ladder down; cauldron `set` inside; `scatter` mushrooms + moss around
