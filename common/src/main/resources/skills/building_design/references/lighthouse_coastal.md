# Style: lighthouse_coastal — 海岸灯塔

One landmark cylinder with stripes and a glowing crown.

## Proportions
- tower: `cylinder` hollow r=3, height 14-18; keeper's cottage 7x5 walls 3 at the base

## Palette
- stripes: alternate 3-high bands of white_concrete and red_concrete on the tower
- crown: glass `walls` ring + polished_blackstone cap; light: sea_lantern core
- cottage: cobblestone + oak, small `roof`

## Recipe
1. hollow cylinder in 3-high color bands (separate cylinder ops per band)
2. top: 1 overhanging disc (`cylinder` r=4 h=1), glass ring `walls` 2 high, sea_lantern
   cluster in the center, capped by a slab dome (2 shrinking discs)
3. spiral ladder inside; cottage attached at the base with its own gable `roof`
