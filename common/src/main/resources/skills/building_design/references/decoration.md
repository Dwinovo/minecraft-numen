# Decoration recipes — 让房子像有人住

Small ops snippets to run AFTER the shell. Pick 3-5 per build, not all.

- window dressing: trapdoor shutters beside windows; flower_pot or lantern on `set` sills
- doorstep: 1 stone step + 2 lanterns flanking the door; path `scatter` (coarse_dirt/gravel/path)
- garden: `scatter` short_grass + 2-3 flower types at density 0.15-0.3 around the house
- chimney: 1x1 column past the ridge, campfire on top (smoke!)
- interior minimum: bed, crafting_table, furnace, torch x2 — `set` along walls, never mid-room
- fence yard: `walls`-shaped fence rect with a gap or fence_gate op via `set`
- lighting rule: no dark corners — a light source every 6 blocks indoors, at the door outside
- roof interest: a `set` lightning_rod or bell near the ridge; lantern under eave corners
