# Style: chinese — 中式(民居/园林)

Symmetry on axis, red columns, sweeping tiled roofs, walled courtyards.

## Proportions
- main hall 13x9 walls 4; side halls mirror-symmetric; courtyard enclosed by 2-high wall
- roof taller than Japanese: ridge height ~ half of footprint depth

## Palette
- columns: `red terracotta`? better: mangrove_planks/logs for red-brown columns; walls: white_concrete
- roof: gray — deepslate_tiles/polished_deepslate; base: polished_andesite platform
- gold accents: sparse `waxed_exposed_copper`? keep minimal; light: red lantern (lantern + red banner)

## Recipe
1. platform `box` polished_andesite, 1 high, 1 wider than the hall all round
2. columns: `set` mangrove_log at 2-block rhythm along the front face
3. `walls` white_concrete behind the column line; `roof` deepslate over an extended rect
4. ridge decoration: `set` stone_brick_wall blocks along the ridge line ends

## Signature details
- courtyard moon gate: circular opening in the yard wall (carve with air `sphere` hollow trick)
- garden: `scatter` bamboo (needs dirt), azalea, a small `box` water basin is SKIPPED (liquids
  are skipped) — use blue stained glass as stylized water instead
