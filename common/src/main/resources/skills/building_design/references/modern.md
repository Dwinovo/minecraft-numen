# Style: modern — 现代极简

Flat planes, glass walls, cantilevers, monochrome.

## Proportions
- stacked rectangles: ground floor 13x9 h4, upper floor offset/cantilevered 2 blocks
- full-height glass on 1-2 faces; flat roofs, NO visible pitched roof ever

## Palette
- primary: white_concrete + gray_concrete; floor: smooth_stone/polished_deepslate
- glass: glass (full block) walls; trim: iron/blackstone; light: end_rod, sea_lantern in floor

## Recipe
1. `box` slab floors 1-thick; `walls` concrete on closed faces; glass `walls` on open faces
2. cantilever: upper `box` floor extends past the lower footprint (flat, no supports)
3. roof terrace: parapet `walls` 1 high; pool is SKIPPED (liquids) — tinted_glass instead
4. `set` end_rod rows for lighting lines; wide steps: 3-wide smooth_stone slabs
