# Style: japanese — 和风(民居/神社)

Horizontal elegance: dark frame, white infill, wide low roofs with lifted eaves.

## Proportions
- footprint wide and shallow (13x9), walls exactly 3 tall; roof LOW pitch and WIDE
- raised floor: foundation 1 above ground, steps in front; verandas 2 deep on the long side

## Palette
- frame: dark_oak_log / spruce_log posts and beams; infill: white — use `white_concrete`
  or `smooth_quartz`; floor: spruce_planks; roof: deepslate_tiles or dark_prismarine
- accents: red — `red_terracotta`? sparingly (torii, trim); light: lantern on fence posts

## Roof recipe
`roof` over a rect EXTENDED 2 beyond the walls each side (wide eaves); for lifted corners,
after the roof op `set` upside-down stairs (half=top) at the four eave corners.

## Signature details
- torii gate out front: two dark_oak columns + double crossbeam, `set` piece by piece
- veranda: spruce_planks platform 1 high + fence railing
- shoji grid: replace some wall cells with white pane + dark frame rhythm (alternate set)
- stone path: `scatter` stone/andesite to the entrance; stone lantern: cobblestone wall block + slab + torch

## Avoid
- tall walls; steep roofs; cobblestone anywhere visible
