# Villagers and trading (1.21.1)

## Professions

An unemployed villager takes the profession of a free **job site block** near it. Until it has traded once, breaking its block and placing another changes its trades.

| Job site block | Profession | Typical trades |
|---|---|---|
| Composter | Farmer | Buys wheat, potatoes, carrots, beetroot; sells bread, golden carrots, glistering melon |
| Lectern | Librarian | Sells enchanted books (any non-treasure enchantment, also **Mending**), bookshelves, lanterns, name tags |
| Smithing table | Toolsmith | Sells iron and diamond tools (enchanted at higher levels) |
| Grindstone | Weaponsmith | Sells iron and diamond swords and axes |
| Blast furnace | Armorer | Sells chainmail, iron and diamond armor, shields |
| Smoker | Butcher | Buys raw meat; sells cooked meat |
| Barrel | Fisherman | Buys fish and string; sells cooked fish, enchanted fishing rods |
| Fletching table | Fletcher | Buys sticks, flint, string, feathers; sells bows, arrows, crossbows |
| Brewing stand | Cleric | Buys rotten flesh, gold; sells redstone, lapis, glowstone, ender pearls |
| Cartography table | Cartographer | Sells maps, including explorer maps to monuments, woodland mansions and trial chambers |
| Cauldron | Leatherworker | Sells leather armor, **saddles** |
| Loom | Shepherd | Buys wool and dyes; sells wool, beds, banners |
| Stonecutter | Mason | Buys clay and stone; sells bricks, terracotta, quartz |

Nitwits (green robes) never take a job. Wandering traders appear near players with exotic goods.

## How trading works

- **Emeralds** are the currency. Villagers buy common items for them (sticks, coal, paper, wheat, string …) and sell useful ones.
- Each trade earns the villager experience; it levels up from Novice through Apprentice, Journeyman and Expert to Master and unlocks two more trades per level.
- Trades lock after a number of uses and restock when the villager works at its job site block (up to twice a day).
- Prices go up when a trade is heavily used, and **down** for players with good reputation: curing a zombie villager or having the Hero of the Village effect makes trades much cheaper.

## Villages

- Villages generate in plains, desert, savanna, taiga and snowy plains, with houses, beds, farms, a bell, and job site blocks.
- Villagers **breed** when there are free beds and they have enough food (bread, carrots, potatoes or beetroot, which farmer villagers share).
- **Iron golems** (100 HP) spawn in villages with enough villagers; they attack hostile mobs and anyone who hurts a villager, and drop 3-5 iron ingots.
- At night villagers go to bed and stay inside; zombies attack them.

## Zombie villagers

- A villager killed by a zombie may turn into a zombie villager (always on Hard, 50% on Normal).
- **Curing**: throw a splash potion of Weakness at it and feed it a **golden apple**; it shakes and becomes a villager again after a few minutes (keep it out of the sun). Cured villagers give big discounts.

## Raids

- Killing a raid captain (a pillager wearing a banner, found at outposts and in patrols) drops an **ominous bottle**; drinking it gives **Bad Omen**. Entering a village with Bad Omen turns it into Raid Omen, and a raid starts shortly after.
- A raid is several waves of pillagers, vindicators, evokers, witches and ravagers. Winning gives the **Hero of the Village** effect (big discounts).
- Milk removes Bad Omen.
