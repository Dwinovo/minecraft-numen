# Game progression (1.21.1)

Survival progresses through materials: each tier of tool can harvest the next tier's ore, and each dimension needs things from the previous one. Details of each step live in `mining_and_ores`, `crafting_and_smelting`, `the_nether` and `the_end`.

## Tiers and what they unlock

| Tier | Needs | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Wood | logs (by hand) | planks, sticks, crafting table, wooden pickaxe → stone and coal |
| Stone | cobblestone (wooden pickaxe) | stone tools, furnace → iron, copper, lapis ore; charcoal and cooked food |
| Iron | raw iron smelted (stone pickaxe) | iron pickaxe → diamond, gold, redstone; bucket, shield, iron armor, flint and steel, compass, rails |
| Diamond | diamond ore (iron pickaxe), mostly near Y -58 | diamond pickaxe → obsidian; enchanting table; diamond armor |
| Netherite | ancient debris in the Nether (diamond pickaxe) + templates from bastions | netherite gear (strongest, lava-proof items) |

## Milestones and their requirements

| Milestone | Requires | Where the materials come from |
|---|---|---|
| Nether portal | 10 obsidian, flint and steel (or fire charge) | Obsidian: diamond pickaxe on obsidian, or a ruined portal; water poured over lava sources makes it. Flint: gravel |
| Blaze rods | entering the Nether, finding a fortress | Blazes at fortress spawners; 0-1 rod each |
| Ender pearls | killing endermen, or bartering gold with piglins | Endermen: night overworld, End, densest in warped forests |
| Eyes of ender | 1 pearl + 1 blaze powder each; up to 12 to fill a portal | 6 rods give 12 powder; frames are 10% pre-filled each |
| Finding a stronghold | eyes thrown toward it, or knowing its location | Closest ring is 1280-2816 blocks from (0, 0) |
| Activating the End portal | all 12 frames filled | |
| Ender dragon | destroying the 10 end crystals, then 200 HP of damage | Bow and arrows for the crystals; blocks to climb caged pillars and bridge to the island |

Things that make the late game easier: iron or diamond armor, a bow with plenty of arrows, a stack of cooked food, building blocks for bridging and pillaring, a bucket of water (overworld and End), golden apples, Fire Resistance potions in the Nether, gold armor around piglins.

## Vanilla advancements along the way

| Advancement | Earned by |
|---|---|
| Stone Age | mining stone with the new pickaxe |
| Getting an Upgrade | a stone pickaxe |
| Acquire Hardware | smelting an iron ingot |
| Suit Up | wearing any iron armor piece |
| Isn't It Iron Pick | an iron pickaxe |
| Diamonds! | picking up a diamond |
| We Need to Go Deeper | entering the Nether |
| A Terrible Fortress | entering a Nether fortress |
| Into Fire | picking up a blaze rod |
| Eye Spy | entering a stronghold |
| The End? | entering the End |
| Free the End | killing the ender dragon |
| The Next Generation | holding the dragon egg |
