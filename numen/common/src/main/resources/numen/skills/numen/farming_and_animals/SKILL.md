# Farming and animals (1.21.1)

## Farmland

- A **hoe** turns dirt, grass, dirt path or coarse dirt into farmland (coarse dirt becomes dirt first).
- Farmland is **hydrated** by water within 4 blocks horizontally on the same level or one above; hydrated farmland grows crops faster. Dry farmland with nothing planted turns back to dirt.
- Jumping or landing on farmland tramples it back to dirt; walking does not.
- Crops need **light level 9 or more** at the crop to keep growing (sunlight or torches), and break if their farmland turns to dirt.
- **Bone meal** (from bones: 1 bone → 3 bone meal) advances a crop several stages per use.

## Crops

| Crop | Planted from | Harvest | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat | wheat seeds (from breaking grass) | 1 wheat + 0-3 seeds | 8 growth stages; 3 wheat → bread |
| Carrot | carrot | 2-5 carrots | Carrots and potatoes are their own seed |
| Potato | potato | 2-5 potatoes, 2% poisonous potato | Baked potato is the food |
| Beetroot | beetroot seeds | 1 beetroot + seeds | 6 beetroot → beetroot soup |
| Pumpkin / melon | seeds on farmland | a fruit block on an empty dirt, grass or farmland block next to the stem | The stem stays and fruits again; melon block → 3-7 slices |
| Sugar cane | sugar cane on sand, dirt or grass directly beside water | grows to 3 tall; break the upper parts to keep the base | Paper (3 cane → 3 paper), sugar |
| Cactus | on sand, with nothing solid beside it | up to 3 tall | Hurts on touch; smelts to green dye |
| Cocoa beans | on jungle logs | 2-3 beans | |
| Sweet berries | bush on grass or dirt | berries; the bush scratches | |
| Nether wart | soul sand, any light, any dimension | 2-4 wart | Found in fortresses; brewing base |
| Bamboo | dirt or sand | grows very fast | Scaffolding, sticks, fuel |
| Kelp | under water | grows toward the surface | Dried kelp is food and, as a block, fuel |

## Breeding

Feeding two adults of the same kind their breeding food makes them pair up and produce a baby. Parents can breed again after 5 minutes; a baby grows up in 20 minutes (feeding it speeds that up). Holding the breeding food makes the animals follow.

| Animal | Breeding food |
|---|---|
| Cow, mooshroom, sheep, goat | Wheat |
| Pig | Carrot, potato, beetroot |
| Chicken | Any seeds (wheat, melon, pumpkin, beetroot, torchflower, pitcher pod) |
| Rabbit | Dandelion, carrot, golden carrot |
| Horse, donkey (tamed) | Golden apple, golden carrot |
| Llama (tamed) | Hay bale |
| Wolf (tamed) | Any meat |
| Cat (tamed), ocelot | Raw cod, raw salmon |
| Fox | Sweet berries, glow berries |
| Turtle | Seagrass |
| Bee | Flowers |
| Panda | Bamboo (with bamboo nearby) |
| Frog | Slimeball |
| Axolotl | Bucket of tropical fish |
| Camel | Cactus |

| Hoglin | Crimson fungus |
| Strider | Warped fungus |

## Taming

| Animal | How |
|---|---|
| Wolf | Bones until hearts appear (each bone ~33%); then follows, sits, and fights for its owner |
| Cat | Raw cod or salmon, approached slowly |
| Parrot | Seeds (never cookies — poisonous) |
| Horse, donkey, mule, llama | Mount repeatedly until it stops throwing the rider; a saddle (loot or trade only) lets a horse be steered |

## Products

- **Wool**: shears give 1-3 wool and the sheep regrows it by eating grass. Sheep can be dyed.
- **Eggs**: chickens lay one every 5-10 minutes; a thrown egg sometimes hatches a chick.
- **Milk**: a bucket on a cow or goat; drinking it clears status effects.
- **Honey and honeycomb**: from full beehives with a glass bottle or shears; a campfire under the hive keeps the bees calm.
- **Leather** from cows, horses, llamas, hoglins; 4 rabbit hides also craft into leather.
