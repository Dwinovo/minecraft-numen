# Mobs and combat (1.21.1)

## Weapons

| Weapon | Damage | Attacks per second |
|---|---|---|
| Hand | 1 | 4 |
| Sword: wood / gold, stone, iron, diamond, netherite | 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 | 1.6 |
| Axe: wood / gold, stone, iron, diamond, netherite | 7, 9, 9, 9, 10 | 0.8 to 1.0 |
| Trident (melee / thrown) | 9 / 8 | 1.1 |
| Mace | 6, plus extra damage per block fallen before the hit | 0.6 |
| Bow (fully drawn) | about 6, up to 10 on a critical | |
| Crossbow | 6-11 | slow reload |

- An attack does full damage only after the weapon's cooldown has refilled; swinging early does much less.
- **Critical hit**: attacking while falling (not sprinting) deals 50% more.
- A sword hit while standing still sweeps nearby mobs too.
- **Shields** block attacks from the front (including arrows and most of an explosion). An axe hit disables a shield for 5 s.
- Smite adds damage against undead (zombies, skeletons, wither skeletons, the Wither, phantoms, drowned); Bane of Arthropods against spiders, silverfish, endermites and bees.
- Fire Aspect and Flame have no effect on fire-immune mobs (blazes, magma cubes, wither skeletons, ghasts, striders, zombified piglins).

Armor points (20 = full diamond) reduce most attack damage; toughness (diamond, netherite) keeps that reduction against big hits.
