# The Nether (1.21.1)

## Portals

- A frame is a rectangle of **obsidian** (crying obsidian does not count) at least 4 wide and 5 tall, at most 23 x 23; the inside is air. The four corners are optional, so the smallest frame takes **10 obsidian** (14 with corners).
- It is lit by fire inside the frame: **flint and steel** or a fire charge used on the frame's inside, or any fire that lands there. A lit portal fills with purple; standing in it for 4 s changes dimension.
- **Coordinates**: 1 block in the Nether = 8 blocks in the overworld horizontally (x and z divided by 8; y is unchanged). A portal links to the nearest existing portal around the matching position (search radius 128 blocks in the overworld, 16 in the Nether), else it builds a new one there.
- Explosions (ghast fireballs) can put a portal out without breaking the obsidian; it can be lit again.
- **Ruined portals** (overworld and Nether) are broken frames with obsidian and crying obsidian already in them, plus a chest of gold loot. Obsidian otherwise forms where water flows onto a lava source.

## How the Nether differs

- The space is Y 0 to 127 with bedrock floor and ceiling (the roof is at 123-127). A **lava sea** fills everything below Y 31; most ground is netherrack over it.
- **Water cannot exist**: a water bucket evaporates. Ice broken here leaves no water.
- **Beds explode** when used (a strong blast that sets fires). A respawn anchor charged with glowstone sets the respawn point instead.
- Lava flows farther and faster (7 blocks). Netherrack, once lit, burns forever.
- Compasses and clocks spin; maps still work.
- Items dropped into lava are destroyed, except netherite items, which float.

## Biomes

| Biome | Looks like | Notable |
|---|---|---|
| Nether wastes | red netherrack, lava falls | zombified piglins, ghasts, nether gold and quartz |
| Crimson forest | red fungus trees, crimson nylium | hoglins and piglins; hoglins are the steady meat source |
| Warped forest | teal fungus trees, warped nylium | endermen in numbers, almost no other hostiles — the calmest biome |
| Soul sand valley | soul sand and soul soil, bone fossils | skeletons and ghasts; soul sand slows walking |
| Basalt deltas | basalt and blackstone pillars | magma cubes; the roughest terrain |

## Nether fortress

- Dark **nether brick** corridors and bridges on stilts, often over the lava sea; it spreads over a wide area and many heights.
- **Blaze spawners** (usually two, in small rooms on the bridges or at stair landings) and wandering blazes; **wither skeletons** in the corridors.
- **Nether wart** grows on soul sand in its stairwells (the brewing base ingredient).
- Chests: diamonds, iron, gold, saddles, horse armor, obsidian, flint and steel.
- A blaze rod drops only when a player (or a tamed wolf) killed the blaze; 0-1 per blaze, Looting adds more. One rod grinds into 2 blaze powder.

## Bastion remnant

- Huge **blackstone** and gilded blackstone buildings with gold blocks, guarded by piglins and **piglin brutes** (always hostile, 50 HP), and hoglins in the stables.
- Four layouts: housing units, hoglin stables, bridge, and the **treasure room** (a lava-moat room with gold blocks and a magma cube spawner).
- Chests hold gold, ancient debris, netherite scrap, crying obsidian, enchanted gear, and **netherite upgrade smithing templates** (always one in the treasure chest; 10% in the other chests). Templates are found nowhere else.

## Piglins and gold

- Wearing **any one piece of gold armor** keeps ordinary piglins neutral. Even then they attack anyone who opens or breaks a chest, barrel or shulker box, or mines gold ore, gold blocks or gilded blackstone near them. Brutes ignore gold.
- **Bartering**: drop or hand a gold ingot to an adult piglin; it studies it for 6 s and throws something back. About 2% of barters give 2-4 ender pearls; others give obsidian, crying obsidian, fire resistance potions, string, quartz, iron nuggets, soul sand, gravel, blackstone and similar.
- Piglins pick up gold items lying on the ground. Piglins taken out of the Nether turn into zombified piglins after 15 s.

## Ancient debris and netherite

| Step | Facts |
|---|---|
| Ancient debris | Y 8 to 119, by far most common at Y 8-24 (peak ~15); 1-3 block veins that **never touch air**, so it is only found by digging. Needs a diamond or netherite pickaxe; hard (slow to mine) and blast-proof, so TNT and bed explosions clear netherrack around it without destroying it. |
| Netherite scrap | Smelt 1 ancient debris → 1 scrap |
| Netherite ingot | 4 netherite scrap + 4 gold ingots (shapeless) → 1 ingot |
| Upgrade | Smithing table: netherite upgrade template + one diamond tool or armor piece + 1 netherite ingot → the netherite version; enchantments and durability carry over; the template is used up |
| More templates | 1 template + 7 diamonds + 1 netherrack (the block it is copied from) → 2 templates |

A full netherite armor set therefore needs 4 ingots (16 debris and 16 gold ingots), 4 templates (1 found and 3 copies, 21 diamonds) and a diamond set to upgrade (24 diamonds).

## Other Nether resources

- **Glowstone** clusters hang from the ceiling (drop glowstone dust; Fortune helps).
- **Nether quartz ore** (quartz, experience) and **nether gold ore** (gold nuggets) are everywhere in netherrack.
- **Magma cream** from magma cubes, **ghast tears** from ghasts, **crimson and warped fungus**, **shroomlight**, **soul sand** and **soul soil** (soul torches, soul campfires).
- **Striders** walk on lava and can be ridden with a saddle, steered by a warped fungus on a stick.
