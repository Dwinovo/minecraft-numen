# steampunk — 蒸汽朋克

Victorian brick with the machinery left on the outside. Pipes, boilers, gauges
and gearwheels are bolted onto and through buildings that would otherwise be
ordinary, and copper is everywhere because it oxidises into that green nobody
else has. Everything looks retrofitted — the machine came after the building and
was never tidied away.

## Materials
- **body**: bricks, deepslate_bricks, polished_blackstone, dark_oak_planks
- **metal**: copper_block and all its weathered stages, oxidized_copper,
  cut_copper_stairs — copper is the signature and should be everywhere
- **pipes and frame**: iron_bars, chain, iron_block, lightning_rod, hopper,
  cauldron, blast_furnace
- **glass**: tinted_glass, brown_stained_glass in small round-ish ports
- **light**: lantern, redstone_lamp, campfire venting through a stack
- **gauges and gears**: item_frame with a compass or clock, note_block, observer

## Proportions
- vertical and cluttered: towers and stacks rather than long low masses
- chimneys and pipe runs rising 1.5–2 times the roof height
- machinery covers 0.2–0.4 of the visible facade
- windows small and round-ish, punched into brick

## Signature moves
- **External pipework** — runs of iron bars and chains climbing the outside,
  turning corners, disappearing into walls. The more it looks retrofitted, the
  better.
- **Copper everywhere** — domes, gutters, boiler shells, all in mixed weathering
  states so some is bright and some is green.
- **Boiler and stack** — a fat copper cylinder with a tall chimney and a fire
  visible at the base.
- **Rivet bands** — rows of buttons or small blocks along a metal seam.

## Variants
- **airship dock** — mooring mast, gantries, a balloon overhead
- **workshop** — cluttered interior, gears turning, tools on every wall
- **clocktower** — brick tower, huge copper clock face, exposed mechanism
- **factory town** — several stacks, pipes bridging between buildings

## Avoid
- clean minimal surfaces
- modern materials like concrete or large plate glass
- hiding the machinery inside
